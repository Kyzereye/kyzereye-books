# Introduction (Option B) — Outline

**Status:** Outline — build from here  
**Prose draft:** `introduction.md`  
**Role:** Orient the reader. Pattern is primary. Bible is the main developed resource. Other ancient texts appear where they state a principle more clearly.

**Note:** Root `../introduction.md` is the Bible-led intro draft. This track does not replace it.

---

## Purpose

- Name the pattern (not one religion) as the subject of the book  
- Show that LoA branding is modern while the pattern is old  
- Place early 1900s teachers in the middle of that timeline  
- State that older wisdom teaches the pattern, with the Bible as the main resource in depth  
- Non-conversion stance  
- Map the book and hand off to Chapter 1  

**Not in this chapter:** Full principle teaching (Ch 1); full verse chains; full multi-religion survey  

---

## Opening — The pattern looks modern

- People talk about LoA as if it were modern  
- Basic ideas: ask, believe, Emotional agreement, watch words, gratitude, power within  
- Famous in our lifetime → easy to think it started recently  

---

## Middle layer — Early 1900s

- Hill, Wattles, Trine, Atkinson / New Thought  
- Later: Neville and similar  
- Names change; core teaching stays familiar  

---

## Older layer — The pattern is ancient

- Not only the Bible  
- Mind, word, desire, focus, return appear in Buddhist, Hindu, Hermetic, Stoic, Egyptian, Taoist, and biblical wisdom  
- This book does not tour every religion equally  
- It follows the **pattern**, develops the **Bible** as the main resource, and brings in other texts where they are clearer  

**Core line:**  
The subject is the pattern. The Bible is the main book we work through in depth.

---

## What this book is / is not

**Is:**  
- Observation and comparison  
- Pattern-first map  
- Context-respecting use of sources  

**Is not:**  
- Conversion to Christianity, LoA, or a hybrid  
- A claim that all religions teach the same religion  
- Proof-texting or out-of-context quotes  

---

## Two (or more) ways to hold the pattern

- Law built into reality  
- Personal God / relational answer  
- Other frames exist (Tao, mind-only, etc.) — named lightly; explored more in Ch 12  
- Reader need not settle before starting
