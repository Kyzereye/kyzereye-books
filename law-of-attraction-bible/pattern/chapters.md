## Chapter 1 — The Principles of the Law of Attraction

**Job:** Put the LoA map on the table before ancient texts. Shared vocabulary only.  
**Outline:** `chapter-01-outline.md` · **Prose:** `chapter-01.md`

**Stance:** Not converting anyone to LoA, Christianity, or any hybrid. Observation of a pattern.

### The ten principles

1. **Like attracts like** — Dominant thoughts, feelings, and beliefs draw matching circumstances.  
2. **Inner creates outer** — Reality mirrors consciousness. Change the inner state; the outer rearranges.  
3. **Ask / desire** — Get clear on what you want. Ask.  
4. **Believe / assume** — Hold it as already true (or inevitable). Faith sets the result.  
5. **Emotional agreement** — Emotion is the fuel. The feeling of already having it matters.  
6. **Focus / attention** — What you dwell on expands. Obsessing over lack keeps lack.  
7. **Words and imagination create** — Speech, visualization, and identity statements shape what shows up.  
8. **Gratitude and alignment** — Thankfulness and a receiving state open the way; force and desperation close it.  
9. **Allow / don’t block** — Release attachment to how and when. Doubt, resistance, and contradiction interfere.  
10. **You are the operant power** — Creative agency is in you (universe / Source / God-within) — not begging from powerlessness.
