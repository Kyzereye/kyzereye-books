## Purpose

- State what the Law of Attraction claims, in plain language  
- Show the ten principles as one system, not ten separate tips

---

## Opening

- The Introduction asked what the moves are  
- This chapter answers in LoA language only  
- Teachers differ in vocabulary (vibration, assumption, ask/believe/receive) but share a skeleton  
- Brief nod to early 1900s and popular teaching — recognition, not history essay  

---

## Section A — What people mean by Law of Attraction

- Inner life and outer life are linked  
- Like returns like  
- Common popular sequence: ask, believe, receive  
- Same skeleton under different teachers:
  - Popular LoA / *The Secret*-style teaching → ask, believe, receive  
  - Abraham-Hicks → vibration and allowing  
  - Neville → imagination and assumption  
  - Earlier New Thought / Hill / Wattles → thought, desire, faith, definite aim  

---

## Section B — The ten principles

For each: name → definition → how it shows up → bridge to the next.

1. Like attracts like → bridge to inner creates outer  
2. Inner creates outer → bridge to ask / desire  
3. Ask / desire → bridge to believe / assume  
4. Believe / assume → bridge to Emotional agreement 
5. Emotional agreement → bridge to focus / attention  
6. Focus / attention → bridge to words and imagination  
7. Words and imagination create → bridge to gratitude and alignment  
8. Gratitude and alignment → bridge to allow / don’t block  
9. Allow / don’t block → bridge to operant power  
10. You are the operant power → bridge to the rest of the book  

---

## Section C — One system

- Pull the ten into one sequence the reader can remember  
- Note that breaking a link (especially belief, focus, or allow) weakens the chain  
- This map is what later chapters will follow through older texts, with the Bible as main resource
