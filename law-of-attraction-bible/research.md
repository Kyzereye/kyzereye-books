# Law of Attraction and the Bible — Research Notes

Working thesis: Scripture (or large parts of it) describes the same operating pattern the Law of Attraction names — ask, believe, speak, focus, gratitude, inner state → outer result — whether that pattern is an impersonal “law” or the way reality works under God.

Two framings to hold in tension:

1. **Manuscript of a law** — the Bible teaches a consistent inner → outer mechanism.
2. **Designed reality under God** — same mechanism, but relational (prayer, faith, covenant), not mere vibration.

## Author stance

This book is **not** trying to convert anyone — not to Christianity, not to LoA, not to a hybrid religion.

It is an **observation**: a repeating pattern in Scripture matches the principles of the Law of Attraction. The Bible seems to teach that pattern — and can even be read as a kind of **handbook** for it.

- Job of the book: map the overlap so readers can see what the author noticed
- Tone: compare / illuminate — not evangelize, recruit, or initiate
- Reader keeps their own conclusions about God, the universe, and what to do with the pattern

Two framings still held in tension (see thesis above): impersonal law vs designed reality under God. Either way, the operating pattern is the subject.

### Discovery, not monopoly (author thought — parked)

These principles were not invented by one person, group, or culture working alone. Many cultures were probably already thinking them in some form at some stage. The pattern was “out there” in the cosmos for humans to discover. It was only a matter of time — maybe centuries — for the pieces to show up in different places and later be collected into one map (what modern LoA names).

- Cultures are **witnesses** to the pattern, not sole inventors of a private secret
- Parallel appearance across traditions is expected if the pattern is real and discoverable
- Collection into one labeled system is late; the operating pieces are old

**Useful later (when drafting):**
- **Introduction** — why the pattern appears across traditions without claiming one stole from another
- **Chapter 12 (where they part)** — discovery vs invention; collection vs monopoly; why overlap does not erase real differences

### The book is a story, not a list (keep in mind)

These ten principles are not ten separate tips. They are one process. Each chapter should pick up a question the last one could not finish — not start a new encyclopedia entry. Chapter 1’s “One system” already names the chain. Later chapters (and each “The next move” close) should keep telling that story.

**Story-spine**

Matching raises a question: if like returns like, **where is the seed held?** → Inner.  
Inner without a target is fog: **what do you actually want?** → Ask.  
An ask without holding the end is a wish: **do you treat it as real?** → Believe.  
Belief without feeling stays thin: **does the body agree?** → Emotional agreement.  
Feeling dies if attention feeds the opposite: **what are you feeding?** → Focus.  
Focus takes form: **what are you saying and picturing?** → Words and imagination.  
Strong tools fail in panic: **what posture are you in?** → Gratitude / alignment.  
Then it needs room: **are you still stirring the pot?** → Allow.  
Under all of it: **who is driving?** → Operant power.

That is the plot. Witnesses and verses are scenes. If a quote doesn’t move that plot, it’s decoration.

**How each chapter should feel**

1. **Open** — we are here because the last chapter forced this question.
2. **Develop** — this principle, with texts, context, contrast.
3. **Close (The next move)** — this principle cannot finish the job; here is the missing piece.

Example: Chapter 2’s next move is the model — matching is established, so the story has to go inside. Chapter 3 should not reopen “is matching ancient?” It should start from **seed is inside**, then ask what that inner life actually is.

**While reading primary sources, ask two questions of every passage**

- Does this teach **this** principle, or the neighbor? (Some texts sit on a fence — e.g. Hermetic correspondence between Ch 2 matching and Ch 3 inner/outer.)
- Does this **hand off** — does it leave a question the next chapter must answer?

**Test:** Could someone skip the verse list and still feel **why this chapter had to come next**? If yes, it’s a story. If no, it’s a list.

## Ancient parallels (LoA pattern is old; the label is modern)

**Intro line to keep:** People talk about the Law of Attraction as if it were modern. The branding is (*The Secret*, New Thought, late 19th–20th century). The **pattern** — mind, word, desire, focus, return — is older.

This book’s lens stays the Bible. Non-biblical sources below are for later use (Introduction color, footnotes, “not only one tradition”) — not a world-religion detour.

### Closest pattern matches

| Source | Rough date | LoA-like claim |
|---|---|---|
| **Dhammapada** (Buddhism) | ~3rd c. BCE core | Mind precedes all things; what you think, you become; suffering follows an impure mind like the cart-wheel |
| **Upanishads** (desire/identity texts) | ~800–300 BCE | Deep desire shapes what you become; self/Atman as inner reality |
| **Bhagavad Gita** | ~200 BCE–200 CE | Focus, attachment, desire, detachment (*karma yoga* / non-clinging ≈ allow) |
| **Corpus Hermeticum** | ~1st–3rd c. CE Egypt/Greek | Mind as creative; “like to like”; human as divine mind in form |
| **Emerald Tablet** (Hermetic) | Medieval Latin form; Hermetic tradition older | “As above, so below” — inner/outer correspondence |
| **Stoics** (Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius) | 1st–2nd c. CE | Assent, judgment, and inner discourse shape experience; attend to what you hold in mind |
| **Neoplatonism** (Plotinus) | 3rd c. CE | Soul/imagination participates in forming reality; contemplate → become |
| **Proverbs / Wisdom literature** (Bible + ANE cousins) | Iron Age → Hellenistic | Heart, tongue, sow/reap |
| **Egyptian heka** | Pharaonic | Creative speech / “words of power”; saying and making linked |
| **Tao Te Ching** | ~4th–3rd c. BCE (trad.) | Alignment, non-forcing (*wu wei*) ≈ allow / don’t block |

### Also relevant (handle dates honestly)

- **Sefer Yetzirah** — creation through letters/speech (Jewish mysticism; final form later, tradition claims antiquity)
- **Zoroastrian** ethics — good/evil thoughts, words, deeds as a linked triad
- **Mesopotamian** ritual/incantation texts — spoken word as effective force
- **I Ching** — reality as patterned; alignment with the pattern matters more than forcing

### Do not call ancient

- **The Kybalion** (1908) — New Thought dressed as “Hermetic.” Useful for LoA history; not an ancient witness.
- **The Secret** / Abraham-Hicks / Neville — modern teachers of an old pattern.

### How to use in the Introduction (short)

> People talk about the Law of Attraction as if it were modern. The label is. The pattern — mind, word, desire, focus, and return — shows up in Buddhist, Hindu, Hermetic, Stoic, Egyptian, and biblical wisdom long before the phrase existed.

Then stay with the Bible as this book’s lens. Best short non-Bible exhibits if needed: **Dhammapada 1** and Hermetic **“like to like.”**

## Tier 1 — Near-identical mechanics

### 1. Believe you already have it
- **Mark 11:24** — “Whatever you ask in prayer, believe that you have received it, and it will be yours.”
- Closest single verse to classic LoA: ask → assume the end → receive
- Pair with **Mark 11:23** (speak to the mountain; do not doubt) and **Matthew 21:22**

### 2. Ask → given
- **Luke 11:9–10** (also Matt 7:7–8) — ask / seek / knock → given / found / opened
- Lead with Luke, not Matthew, for the “ask and it is given” chapter
- Persistence: **Luke 11:5–8**; relationship: **Luke 11:11–13**

### 3. According to your faith
- **Matthew 9:29** — “According to your faith be it unto you.”
- **Matthew 8:13** — “As you have believed, so be it done for you.”
- **Matthew 9:22 / Mark 5:34** — “Your faith has made you well.”

### 4. What you fear comes
- **Job 3:25** — “The thing I greatly feared has come upon me.”

### 5. Doubt / double-mindedness cancels the ask
- **James 1:6–8** — ask in faith, no doubting; double-minded receives nothing
- LoA: mixed vibration / contradicting the desire

### 6. Life and death in the tongue
- **Proverbs 18:21** — death and life in the power of the tongue
- **Mark 11:23** — say to the mountain
- **Numbers 14:28** — “As you have spoken in My hearing, so I will do to you”
- **Job 22:28** — decree a thing and it will be established

### 7. As a man thinks / guard the heart
- **Proverbs 23:7** — as he thinketh in his heart, so is he
- **Proverbs 4:23** — guard the heart; from it flow the springs of life

### 8. Out of the heart
- **Luke 6:45 / Matthew 12:34–35** — abundance of the heart → speech → fruit; good/evil treasure

### LoA mapping
| Biblical claim | LoA equivalent |
|---|---|
| Kingdom / Spirit / Christ within | Power is internal; outer follows inner |
| Image of God | Create by thought/word because like Creator |
| “You are gods” / divine nature | Divine consciousness in form |

### Distinctions (keep sharp)
- **LoA max claim:** You *are* God / Source

### The counterfeit twin
- **Genesis 3:5** — serpent: “You will be like God…”
- Same shape of claim; opposite frame in the text (autonomy as rebellion)
- Put John 10 / Ps 82 / 2 Pet 1:4 / kingdom within facing Gen 3:5 in the book

## Later themes

### True freedom

True freedom is not only the power to choose what you want. It is also ownership of what follows.

In the Law of Attraction pattern, you are the one who gets to name the desire and move toward receiving it. That same position carries responsibility. If you are driving the ship, you are responsible for the course you set, the inner state you practice, and the life that grows from it.

Freedom without responsibility becomes wishful blame. Responsibility without freedom becomes burden. True freedom holds both: you may ask, and you own the ask.
