## Section A — What people mean by “Law of Attraction”

Brief, non-academic:

- Inner state and outer life are linked
- Like returns like (thought, feeling, belief → matching results)
- Ask / believe / receive as a common popular sequence (*The Secret* and related teaching)
- Different teachers, same skeleton:
  - Abraham-Hicks → vibration and allowing
  - Neville Goddard → imagination and assumption
  - Popular LoA → ask, believe, receive / gratitude / visualization

Keep this short. Point is recognition, not a history of New Thought.

## Section B — The ten principles (core of the chapter)

Walk each principle with:

1. **Name**
2. **One-sentence definition**
3. **How it shows up in practice** (2–4 bullets)
4. **How it connects to the next principle** (one line)

### 1. Like attracts like
- Definition: Dominant thoughts, feelings, and beliefs draw matching circumstances
- Practice: What you feed grows; fear and desire both “return”
- Bridge → Inner creates outer: the match starts inside

### 2. Inner creates outer
- Definition: Reality mirrors consciousness; change the inner state first
- Practice: Guard the inner inventory; outer rearrange follows
- Bridge → Ask: clarity of desire gives the inner something to hold

### 3. Ask / desire
- Definition: Get clear on what you want; ask
- Practice: Specific desire beats vague wanting; name the ask
- Bridge → Believe: asking without conviction stays empty

### 4. Believe / assume
- Definition: Hold it as already true (or inevitable); assumption sets the result
- Practice: End-state belief; refuse to live only by current evidence
- Bridge → Emotional agreement: belief wants a felt sense of “done”

### 5. Emotional agreement
- Definition: Emotion is the fuel; the feeling of already having it matters
- Practice: Receiving-state vs desperate hoping; joy/peace/thanks as fuel
- Bridge → Focus: feeling follows what you fix attention on

### 6. Focus / attention
- Definition: What you dwell on expands; obsessing on lack keeps lack
- Practice: Single focus; stop rehearsing the problem all day
- Bridge → Words and imagination: focus becomes speech and mental imagery

### 7. Words and imagination create
- Definition: Speech, visualization, and identity statements shape what shows up
- Practice: Watch the mouth; see the end; “I am” / scene in imagination
- Bridge → Gratitude: creative strain without a receiving posture backfires

### 8. Gratitude and alignment
- Definition: Thankfulness and alignment open the way; force and desperation close it
- Practice: Thanks as if; align with the outcome-state before evidence
- Bridge → Allow: alignment fails if you contradict yourself

### 9. Allow / don’t block
- Definition: Release attachment to how and when; doubt and contradiction interfere
- Practice: No double-mind; persistence without panic; wait without quitting
- Bridge → Operant power: who is doing this — a beggar, or someone with agency?

### 10. You are the operant power
- Definition: Creative agency is in you (universe / Source / God-within) — not begging from powerlessness
- Practice: Live from inner authority / Source-connection, not from helpless pleading
- Bridge → Rest of the book: Does Scripture describe this same operating pattern?

## Section C — One system (short)

Pull the ten into a single sequence the reader can remember:

**Inner state → desire → ask → assume → feel → focus → speak/see → thank/align → allow → from inner power**

Optional simple diagram later (not required for first draft).

Emphasize: break one link and the chain weakens (especially believe, focus, and allow).
