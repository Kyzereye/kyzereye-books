## Purpose

- Name the observation that started the book

- Hand off into Chapter 1 (LoA principles map)

**Not in this chapter:** Full LoA principle teaching (that’s Ch 1); deep verse chains (Ch 2–11); full contrast essay (Ch 12)

---

## Opening — The pattern

- People talk about the Law of Attraction as if it were modern
- The author noticed the same operating pattern showing up in the Bible: ask, believe, speak, focus, gratitude, inner state → outer result
- Not a claim that ancient writers used the phrase “Law of Attraction”
- A claim that the **pattern** is there — and that Scripture can be read as a kind of handbook for it

**Core line:**  
This book follows a pattern the author noticed. It lays that pattern next to the Bible so you can see it too.

---

## What this book is

- Compare / illuminate
- A map of overlap between LoA principles and biblical teaching
- Honest about parallels **and** about differences
- Context-first with Scripture (no verse yanked only because it *sounds* like LoA)

---

## What this book is not

- Not an attempt to convert anyone to Christianity
- Not an attempt to convert anyone to LoA
- Not a new hybrid religion
- Not a demand that you pick “universe” or “God” before you can read further
- Not proof-texting: same words ≠ same teaching

**Stance line (place early, say once cleanly):**  
This book is not trying to convert anyone — not to Christianity, not to LoA. Reader keeps their own conclusions about God, the universe, and what to do with the pattern.

---

## Two ways to hold the pattern (don’t force one)

Keep both on the table:

1. **Manuscript of a law** — the Bible teaches a consistent inner → outer mechanism
2. **Designed reality under God** — same mechanism, but relational (prayer, faith, covenant), not mere vibration

Either way, the **operating pattern** is the subject of the book.

---

## Who this is for

Keep light until audience is locked:

- Readers who know LoA and are curious whether the Bible says anything like it
- Readers who know the Bible and have heard LoA language and want a clear compare
- Anyone interested in how inner life, speech, faith, and outcome are linked

No gatekeeping. No “you must already believe X.”

## How this book is built

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Chapter 1** | The LoA principles — the shared map, before any Bible |
| **Chapters 2–11** | Each principle next to Scripture (verse chains, context kept) |
| **Chapter 12** | Where LoA and the Bible part ways |
| **Chapter 13** | Practice that honors the overlap without lazy syncretism |

### How to read the Scripture chapters

- Verses are a **chain** (anchor → mechanism → story → blocker → contrast), not a pile
- Context before parallel
- Short contrast beats inside chapters; the big parting is Chapter 12
- Suspect or stretchy verses are handled carefully or left out of the main argument

---

## A note on tone

- Direct, clear, non-preachy
- No altar call, no guru initiation
- When LoA and Scripture use different words for a similar move (vibration vs faith/joy/peace), name both and keep moving
