## Chapter 1 — The Principles of the Law of Attraction

**Job of this chapter:** Put the LoA map on the table before any Bible. No conversion. No debate yet. Just the operating principles so later chapters have a shared vocabulary.

**Author stance (carry from research):** This book does not convert anyone to Christianity or to LoA. It maps a pattern the author noticed: Scripture seems to teach these principles — and can be read as a kind of handbook for them.

### The ten principles

1. **Like attracts like** — Dominant thoughts, feelings, and beliefs draw matching circumstances.
2. **Inner creates outer** — Reality mirrors consciousness. Change the inner state; the outer rearranges.
3. **Ask / desire** — Get clear on what you want. Ask.
4. **Believe / assume** — Hold it as already true (or inevitable). Faith sets the result.
5. **Emotional agreement** — Emotion is the fuel. The feeling of already having it matters.
6. **Focus / attention** — What you dwell on expands. Obsessing over lack keeps lack.
7. **Words and imagination create** — Speech, visualization, and identity statements shape what shows up.
8. **Gratitude and alignment** — Thankfulness and a receiving state open the way; force and desperation close it.
9. **Allow / don’t block** — Release attachment to how and when. Doubt, resistance, and contradiction interfere.
10. **You are the operant power** — Creative agency is in you (universe / Source / God-within) — not begging from powerlessness.

## Chapter 2 — Like Attracts Like

**Principle:** Dominant thoughts, feelings, and beliefs draw matching circumstances.

**Context frame for this chapter:** Biblical “like returns like” is usually **moral, relational, and generous** (kind of seed → kind of harvest) — not “think of a car, get a car.” Keep that honesty in the prose.

| Step | Job | Verse / move |
|---|---|---|
| **Anchor** | Return-law named | **Galatians 6:7–8** — Whatever a man sows, that he will also reap; sow to flesh / sow to Spirit (keep v.8 so context stays) |
| **Mechanism** | Mercy and giving return in kind | **Luke 6:37–38** — Judge not / condemn not / forgive / give — and it will be given; measure you use |
| **Mechanism** | Generous sowing → generous reaping | **2 Corinthians 9:6–11** — Sow sparingly / bountifully (collection for the saints — generosity, not wishcraft) |
| **Mechanism** | Generosity and withholding | **Proverbs 11:24–25** — One gives freely yet grows richer; liberal soul will be made fat |
| **Mechanism** | Injustice returns calamity | **Proverbs 22:8** — Whoever sows injustice will reap calamity |
| **Story** | Deceiver reaps deception | **Genesis 27** + **Genesis 29:15–25** — Jacob deceives Isaac; Laban deceives Jacob (narrative like-for-like) |
| **Story** | Trapper caught in own trap | **Psalm 7:14–16** — Makes a pit, falls into the hole he made |
| **Blocker / inverse** | Evil report → matching outcome | **Numbers 13:31–33; 14:1–4, 26–29** — Fear-speech about the land; that generation receives what they said |
| **Contrast** | LoA = vibrational match; Bible = seed returns in *kind* (often ethical). Parallel stands; don’t flatten the difference | |
| **Handoff** | Where is the seed sown? In the heart. → **Chapter 3** | |

## Suspect verses — final call

Removed from the main chapter chains because they are easy to **take out of context** (translation issue, wrong speaker, greeting, judgment oracle, or identity overclaim). You decide: restore, use with a heavy warning, or leave out.

| Verse | Why it’s suspect | If you restore it, only as… |
|---|---|---|
| **Proverbs 23:7** | Famous LoA slogan in KJV; passage is about a stingy host’s false hospitality; many modern translations do **not** read as a universal thought-law | A translation/history footnote — not a pillar |
| **3 John 1:2** | Personal greeting/wish for Gaius — not a universal prosperity decree | Illustration of care for whole-person wellness — not doctrine |
| **Job 3:25** | Job’s lament; the book rejects simplistic “you attracted this” theology | Job’s *experience* of fear — never as God’s endorsed law |
| **Hosea 8:7** | Judgment oracle on Israel’s idolatry/politics (“sow wind, reap whirlwind”) | Example of prophetic judgment — not manifestation technique |
| **Matthew 7:1–2** | “Measure” here is about **judging others**, not general attraction | Only inside “judge not / measure of judgment” |
| **Job 22:28** | Spoken by **Eliphaz**; Job’s friends are later rebuked (Job 42:7) | Do not teach as truth from God’s mouth |
| **Genesis 30:37–43** | Jacob’s rods — folk narrative often forced into visualization doctrine | Optional color story — never a load-bearing claim |
| **Luke 17:20–21** | *Entos hymōn* = within **or** among; about God’s kingdom/reign, not vibration | Discuss translation; don’t hang “you are the power” on it alone |
| **John 10:34–35 + Psalm 82:6** | Jesus’ legal argument; Psalm addresses unjust rulers called *elohim* | History of interpretation — not “everyone is Source” |
| **Exodus 3:14** as human “I AM creates” | God’s name revealed to Moses; Neville-style use is reinterpretation | Parallel in *form* of identity-speech only — label as reinterpretation |
| **1 John 4:17** | “As he is, so are we” — context is **love and confidence in the day of judgment** | Union/love confidence — not manifestation authority |
| **Matthew 5:48** | “Be perfect as your Father” — Sermon on the Mount ethic (love enemies), not divinity claim | Moral likeness — not LoA operant-power proof |
| **Acts 17:28** | Paul quotes Greek poets to pagans: we live *in God* the Creator — not “you are God” | Dependence on God — not identity collapse |
| **Isaiah 55:11** misused as human affirmation | The verse is about **YHWH’s** word succeeding | Pattern humans image — say so; don’t treat as personal decree magic |
| **Psalm 37:4** ripped alone | “Desires of your heart” sits inside trust / dwell / commit / wait (37:3–7) | Only with the full cluster (already how Ch 9 uses it if restored carefully) |

### Still solid, but always write the context (not moved to suspect)

These stayed in the chains because the parallel is real **when the passage’s subject is named**:

- **Galatians 6:7–8** — moral seed (flesh/Spirit)
- **Luke 6:37–38** / **2 Corinthians 9:6–11** — mercy and generosity
- **Mark 11:22–24** — prayer faith *in God*
- **Romans 4:17–21** — Abraham trusting God’s promise
- **Numbers 14** — words judged (dark return-law)
- **Temple / Christ in you texts** — indwelling, not self-deification
